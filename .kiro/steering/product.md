# Product

This repo is a hands-on weather bring-up: wire the S3, climate sensors, display, and SD, flash stage firmware, and write it up as a Zensical walkthrough.

We’re building a local desk climate station (live TFT + CSV on microSD). Not AWS IoT, not a multi-board fleet.

Fleet/AWS stuff belongs elsewhere. Keep the docs visual and phase-gated like the other Ubuntu lab walkthroughs.
